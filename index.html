03 / HOW I WORK

How I manage technical work

In my independent projects, I use Scrum ideas without making the process bigger than the work. I keep a backlog, choose a small Sprint goal, check a working version and use what I learn to plan the next cycle. When the project involves data, I also check sources and totals before sharing a finding.

  1. 01BacklogList the work and set priorities
  2. 02SprintPick one clear, small goal
  3. 03ReviewCheck a version that works
  4. 04Next cycleKeep, fix or change course
